About the consultant

Rooted in the field, connected to the world

Aviprit Vinod Ubarhande is an agricultural consultant specialising in medicinal plants, herbal gardens and agro eco-tourism. Her background is in applied agricultural research. At the ICAR-Central Coastal Agricultural Research Institute (ICAR-CCARI), Goa, she worked as a Young Professional on plant collection and on laying out and managing the institute's thematic medicinal gardens.

She brings that research experience to farmers, landowners and agri-entrepreneurs in India and abroad, helping them turn land into productive, sustainable and visitor-friendly enterprises.

  • Design and management of Navagraha, Rashi and Nakshatra Vatika medicinal gardens at ICAR-CCARI, Goa
  • Herbal garden models for agro-ecotourism units (Dhanvantari Vatika)
  • Medicinal & aromatic plant collection, cultivation and garden planning
  • Coastal and tropical agriculture

Selected publications

  • Dhanvantari Vatika — A Model Herbal Garden for an Agro-Ecotourism Unit (co-author). Transforming Coastal Zone for Sustainable Food and Income Security, Springer, 2022.
  • Astrologically Designed Medicinal Gardens of India, IJBSM, 2021. She is acknowledged for plant collection and garden management.
