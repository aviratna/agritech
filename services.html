FAQ

Common questions

Is the first call free?

Yes — the 30-minute discovery call is free. We use it to understand your goals and recommend the right engagement.

Do you visit farms in person?

Yes, on-site visits are available across India. International clients are usually served remotely, with travel available for larger projects.

How do I pay for a consultation?

Paid sessions can be booked and paid directly through the calendar booking page. Project work is quoted separately after the discovery call.

Can you help with government subsidies?

Yes — we guide you on relevant central and state schemes, and can prepare detailed project reports (DPRs) for bank loans and subsidies.
